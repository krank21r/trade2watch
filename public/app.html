ZoneWatch — live zone monitor

Every entry zone, all assets, one board. Green = long zone live, red = short zone live, amber = waiting.
—
—

Rule of thumb

  • Amber everywhere = do nothing. All assets mid-range — no edge.
  • Red zone live = short setup armed (limit entry, stop above zone).
  • Green zone live = long setup armed (limit entry, stop below zone).
  • Invalidation breached = thesis dead. Close the trade, re-analyze structure.
